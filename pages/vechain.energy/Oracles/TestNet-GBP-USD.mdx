# GBP/USD

Provides the current USD price for GBP to support GBP conversion for USD prices.

## Datasheet

* Address: `0xdcCAaBd81B38e0dEEf4c202bC7F1261A4D9192C6` @ `TestNet`
* Feed ID: `gbp-usd` / `0x6762702d75736400000000000000000000000000000000000000000000000000`
* Heartbeat: `24 Hours`
* Max. Deviation: `1%`
* Update Interval: `5 Minutes`
* Status URL: https://oracle.vechain.energy/gbp-usd
* CCIP Resolver: https://oracle.vechain.energy/gbp-usd/resolver
